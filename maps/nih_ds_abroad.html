DS funding = direct awards (attributed cost of data science projects with a recipient outside the U.S.) + subawards (first-tier amounts that U.S. prime recipients report passing to foreign partners on data science projects). Subaward share = subaward DS funding ÷ DS funding. Color uses a log scale. A project can sit in more than one data science area, so areas add up to more than "All data science".
DS = data science. FY = federal fiscal year (FY2026 preliminary). WHO = World Health Organization regions. FFATA = Federal Funding Accountability and Transparency Act: only subawards of $30,000 or more must be reported, so subaward totals are a floor. Institutes: NCI cancer, NHLBI heart, lung and blood, NIA aging, NIAID allergy and infectious diseases, NICHD child health and human development, NIDA drug abuse, NIDDK diabetes, digestive and kidney diseases, NIGMS general medical sciences, NIMH mental health, NINDS neurological disorders and stroke.
Sources: NIH RePORTER (direct awards), USAspending.gov (subawards), NIH Data Science Taxonomy (labels). Laura Ellis.
