Values are FY2025, the latest full fiscal year; change is since the first year with a value, usually FY2016 (percentage points for shares). FY2026 is preliminary (NIH is still adding records) and is dashed. Institute values use the administering institute, except G1.2, G3.2, and G1.3, which use the institute named as funder on the paper or dataset. For KPIs with several lines, the institute view shows the headline line (C5: AI; G2.2 and G4.2: sum of named resources; G5.3: outside the 50 largest grantee organizations; G5.2 and O1–O5: data science, compared with the institute's other records). O3 has all-NIH values only: its OpenAlex sample is too small to split by institute. With an institute selected, the gray line is all NIH. The table compares institutes on the selected KPI; click a column to sort. Counts cover the full NIH portfolio, not a sample, so there are no sampling intervals; label agreement with hand coding is κ = 0.92 for AI and 0.86 for data science.
DS = data science. AI = artificial intelligence. FY = federal fiscal year. RCDC = Research, Condition, and Disease Categorization. RCR = Relative Citation Ratio. HPC = high-performance computing. FAIR = findable, accessible, interoperable, reusable. IDeA = Institutional Development Award states. K = career development award. LLM = large language model. A record = one project or center component, counted in each year it was funded; funding = extramural attributed cost, center costs counted once.
Sources: NIH RePORTER and ExPORTER (projects, publication, patent, and clinical study links), iCite, Europe PMC, DataCite, OpenAlex, NIH Data Science Taxonomy v0.5 labels. Laura Ellis.
