## Featured project

::: {.callout-note appearance="simple" icon="false"}
### NIH Data Science and AI Taxonomy

How much does NIH invest in data science and AI, and where? I built a taxonomy of data science and AI methods and applied it to NIH grant abstracts with embeddings, clustering, and LLM labeling, then validated the labels against a hand-coded sample and NIH's RCDC categories.

[See the project](skills/nlp-llms.qmd)
:::
